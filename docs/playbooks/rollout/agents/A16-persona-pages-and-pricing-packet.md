# A16 — Persona briefs, pricing/access packet, evidence memo & partner deck outline

**Phase:** 5 Surface · **Owner:** AGENT → OPERATOR (pricing figures and outreach claims need
sign-off) · **Depends on:** O2 (packaging decided or explicitly withheld), A1, A3, A11 ·
**Feeds:** O5, A18, O7 · **Budget:** read-only.

(Prepend `COMMON.md`. Fill placeholders.)

## Objective

Produce the buyer-facing packet the sales/partner motion needs: one brief per ICP, the pricing
page (or the honest reason pricing is withheld), the evidence memo that outreach can attach,
and the outline of the partner deck — all sourced and claim-tagged.

## Inputs

- `A1` (per-segment jobs, pains, buying process, WTP, verbatims), `A3` (competitor price
  evidence), `A11` §6 (message architecture by segment), `O2` (packaging decision, name,
  category), `A10` §3 proofs, `<<STRATEGY_DOCS>>` (unit economics if any), the GO/NO-GO or
  claims-rules register if the project has one.

## Method

1. **Persona brief per ICP** (one page each): who (role, org type, region), the decisive
   question they ask, the job and trigger, current tools and cost, evaluation criteria and
   committee, objections and the answer we can prove, lead message + proof + CTA (from A11),
   what not to say, A1 report ids behind each line. Mark GATED segments.
2. **Pricing page or withholding note.** If O2 froze packaging: tiers, unit, what is
   included, minimums, trial/terms, FAQ, comparison with category norms from A3 (without
   naming vendors unless allowed), and the copy. If O2 withheld pricing: the exact public
   wording, why (one honest sentence), and the date it will be revisited.
3. **Evidence memo** (≤ 2 pages, attachable): what the product is, the proofs with their
   numbers *and* their caveats, a worked example a reader can verify on the public site, who
   it is for, what is asked of a design partner, contact. Every number tagged and sourced.
4. **Partner deck outline** — 10–12 slides: titles, the one message per slide, the proof
   artefact per slide (screenshot, number, example), and which slides are GATED.
5. **Claim table** for the whole packet, as in A15.

## Output — `<<OUT>>/5-surface/A16-personas-pricing-<<DATE>>.md`

COMMON header (Status: SYNTHESIS). Sections 1–5 · Operator sign-off list · Sources.

## Done when

Every ICP has a brief citing A1 ids; the pricing section exists in one of its two forms; the
evidence memo has a verifiable worked example; the claim table is complete.

## Do not

- Do not publish a price the operator has not signed.
- Do not attribute quotes to interviewees by name.
