## Objective

Record how each competitor earns belief: the credibility signals they display, the way they
communicate method and rigour, and the emotional pitch they make to the buyer. This is the
raw material for our own trust architecture (A10/A15). RAW.

## Inputs

- `<<COMPETITORS>>`; A8 screenshots; live browser; vendor "about", "security", "methodology",
  "trust", "resources" pages; footer badges.

## Method

Per vendor, three questions, each answered with evidence:

1. **Trust signals inventory.** Certifications and compliance (SOC 2 type, ISO 27001, FedRAMP,
   GDPR statements) with where displayed; customer logos (count, sectors, government vs
   commercial); named people (leadership bios, former affiliations, analyst bylines); awards,
   analyst-firm mentions, press logos; investor/owner disclosures (is a strong parent used as
   collateral, or hidden?); security/trust centre existence; years-in-business claims.
2. **Tradecraft and method communication.** Does the site name a standard (ICD 203/206,
   Admiralty code, NATO grading, journalistic sourcing norms)? Does it explain source
   grading, confidence language, human review, verification rates, accuracy or latency
   numbers? Quote each claim verbatim and classify: *demonstrated* (a live example, a public
   number with method) vs *asserted* (an adjective). Note anti-claims ("not AI filters",
   "no black box") and what they are positioned against.
3. **Psychological pitch.** In one paragraph with quotes: what fear or aspiration the hero
   sells (be first / don't get blamed / see everything / trust the experts / belong to the
   serious people). Note the register (urgent, calm, institutional, technical) and the
   imagery that carries it (globes, uniforms, dashboards, people).

Then a **cross-vendor table**: vendor · SOC2/ISO/FedRAMP · logo count · named standard ·
demonstrated vs asserted rigour · pitch type.

Add a short section **"Signals nobody in the set uses"** — purely observational (e.g. no one
publishes accuracy; no one names a standard). No recommendation attached.

## Output — `<<OUT>>/2-research/A6-raw-trust-signals-<<DATE>>.md`

COMMON header (Status: RAW). Per-vendor 1–3 · Cross-vendor table · Signals nobody uses ·
Corrections log · Sources. End with: *No strategic synthesis or recommendations in this file.*
