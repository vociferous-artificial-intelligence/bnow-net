# O4 — Identity sign-off

**Phase:** 4 Identity · **Owner:** OPERATOR · **Depends on:** A12 (directions, boards,
tokens) · **Feeds:** A14, A15 · **Time:** 2 hours; optionally one more O3-style round of 3–5
people if two directions are close.

## Why this cannot be delegated

Identity is a taste decision constrained by evidence. The agent has done the constraining
(contrast, licences, scripts, distinctness from the category). Choosing is yours, and it
must be written down so nobody relitigates it in copy or component reviews.

## Preparation

Open the A12 boards side by side, on a laptop and on a phone. Re-read O2's position line and
O3's "their words" section. Look at the A7 cross-vendor table once more so you know what
you are choosing *against*.

## How to decide

Score each direction 1–5 on: fit to position (does the signature device carry the promise?);
distinctness from A7 category patterns; legibility on the densest data view; survival at
phone width; honesty (does it look like what the product is?); practicality (licence cost,
implementation effort, locale coverage). Then decide. If you would show only one to a
prospective customer, that is the answer.

Optional: show two finalists to 3–5 people from the O1 pool for 5 minutes each — "which
company would you trust with a decision, and why?" Record verbatims.
