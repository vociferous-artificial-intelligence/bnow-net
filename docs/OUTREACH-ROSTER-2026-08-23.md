# BNOW.NET — Reviewer & design-partner outreach roster

Compiled 2026-08-23. Companion to `PARTNER-STRATEGY.md`, which owns the strategy; this
file owns the **names, contact routes, and draft correspondence**.

**Nothing here has been sent.** These are drafts for the operator. Agent workstreams are
not authorized to send outreach (`docs/prompts/2026-08-17-roadmap-07…` §Authorization).

**Contact-sourcing rule applied:** every address below comes from an organization's own
public page or the person's own professional site. No data-broker sources (ZoomInfo,
RocketReach, ContactOut, Apollo) were used, and no email pattern was guessed. Where a site
obfuscates staff email, the institutional route is given instead and labelled as such.
Verify before sending — people move.

## Gates that bear on this outreach

Four documented rulings constrain what these emails may say. See
`GO-NO-GO-REGISTER-2026-08-23.md` for the full register.

| Ruling | What it means for an email |
|---|---|
| A18 — endorsement sequencing | Ask for critique. **Never** ask for an endorsement, a quote, or a logo in a first contact. Public naming comes only after they have engaged with the product. |
| A9 — theater-breadth rider | Do not claim depth beyond Russia / Ukraine / Iran. Israel/Gulf is shallow; bh/kw is scaffolded; China is deferred. |
| B1 — OpenSanctions rights | Compliance/sanctions surfaces are beta/internal. Do not describe sanctions screening as a product capability being sold. |
| A12 — beta commercial policy | The words "free" / "no charge" are not approved for publication. "No purchase or card is required to request access" is the approved phrasing. |

One more, from `PARTNER-STRATEGY.md` §6: the sequence is *prepare a 2-page evidence memo →
narrow ask → brutal gaps first, introductions second, endorsement never.* **The memo does
not exist yet.** These warm drafts are written to work without it — they ask for a
conversation, not a review of an attachment — but the memo needs to exist before the
second contact, because that is when they will ask to see something.

## 3. Draft correspondence

Warm and relationship-first, per the chosen posture: lead with why *them*, one sentence on
what this is, ask for a conversation before showing anything. No metrics, no attachment,
no endorsement ask. Subject lines are deliberately plain — this audience deletes anything
that reads like marketing.

Substitute your own signature block. Every draft assumes you send from `go@vociferous.nyc`
or a bnow.net address.

## 5. Before the second contact — what has to exist

1. **The 2-page evidence memo** (`PARTNER-STRATEGY.md` §6.1): scoreboard, source registry,
   claim-source traceability, one RU/UA digest, one Iran/Gulf example, one trade-evasion
   example. Does not exist. Wave 1 works without it; wave 2 will not.
2. **A decision on A8** — automated analyst aid vs analyst-verified. Every one of these
   people will ask some version of "who checks this," and the answer shapes whether you
   are recruiting reviewers or hiring them.
3. **A qualification pass** on the §5 rubric for anyone in §2 before they get an email.
   Buyer trust + regional depth + commercial usefulness, scored 1–5.
4. **A conflict check on Smart** (FARA) and **an explicit decision on Tsukerman's public
   naming**, both before either name appears on anything public.
