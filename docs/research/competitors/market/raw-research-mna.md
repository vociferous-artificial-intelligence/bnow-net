## 1. S-RM — AXA XL Acquisition

### Bottom line
**CONFIRMED (a) — official, on-the-record, dual-sided.** AXA XL announced 6 August 2026 that it has agreed to acquire the remaining shares of S-RM it does not already own, moving from an existing ~49% stake to 100% ownership. **Deal value / purchase price was NOT disclosed by either party or by any source found.** This is a step-up to full ownership of an existing investee, not a fresh outside acquisition of an independent target.

### What was found

**Announcement date:** 6 August 2026 (some secondary outlets date the pickup 7 August 2026).
**Expected closing:** end of September 2026, "subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals" — i.e., as of today (2026-09-07) the deal is signed/announced but likely **not yet closed**.

**Ownership structure (consistent across every source, including both companies' own releases):**
- AXA XL already held **approximately 49%** of S-RM going into this deal.
- AXA XL is acquiring the remaining shares for **100% ownership**.
- S-RM's own release and AXA XL's release both independently describe a **15+ year relationship** between the firms as "both a client and an investor/investee."

**Deal value:** Explicitly stated as **not disclosed** by:
- AXA XL's own press release (axaxl.com)
- S-RM's own press release (s-rminform.com)
- Insurance Journal ("Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.")
- Every other trade-press pickup found (Coverager, Carrier Management, Insurance Business Magazine, Insurance Asia News, Reinsurance News, Business Insurance, Cyber Insurance News, Life Insurance International) — none report a figure; all describe terms as undisclosed.

**Structural/strategic terms:**
- S-RM will operate as part of **AXA XL Risk Advisory**, described as a newly created prevention-focused business unit within AXA XL.
- Executive quotes: Scott Gunter (CEO, AXA XL); Libby Benet (CEO, AXA XL Risk Advisory); Heyrick Bond Gunning (CEO, S-RM) — all on the record in the official releases, consistent across AXA XL's and S-RM's own versions of the release.

**Company background (from releases):** S-RM founded 2005, London HQ, ~400 experts, nine offices across six continents, clients in 140+ countries; provides corporate intelligence, cyber security/incident response, geopolitical intelligence, crisis response, and due diligence.

### What IS confirmed (2021–2022 formation history) — status (a)

**30 June 2021 — Control Risks takes a strategic (minority) equity stake in Geospark Analytics**, NOT a merger or full acquisition:
- Official PR Newswire release: "Control Risks announces strategic investment in Geospark Analytics, harnessing the power of collaborative intelligence."
- **Stake: 10% equity** in Geospark Analytics — explicitly stated in the release.
- Geospark Analytics: founded July 2017, Herndon, VA; maker of the "Hyperion" AI/ML platform for risk/threat monitoring and forecasting (satellite, social media, news, IoT data fusion).
- Quotes on record: Nick Allan (CEO, Control Risks); Omar Balkissoon (Founder, Geospark Analytics).

**August 2022 — Formation/launch of "Seerist"** as a combined platform/brand:
- Control Risks **spun off its own online monitoring platform** and combined it with Geospark's Hyperion technology to launch Seerist.
- Confirmed via multiple independent (a)/(b) sources: Wilson Sonsini law firm's own client-work writeup ("Wilson Sonsini Advises Control Risks on Launch of Seerist"), GovConWire (5 Aug 2022, "Control Risks, Geospark Analytics Join Forces to Create AI-Based Intell Platform"), PR Newswire ("Seerist Establishes the First Augmented Analytics Technology for Security and Threat Intelligence Professionals"), AAP wire pickup, and Seerist's own newsroom.

- **This 2022 event is best read as Geospark Analytics effectively rebranding/consolidating as Seerist**, with Control Risks as an investor/strategic partner contributing its monitoring platform and go-to-market — not two independent companies each fully retained under separate ownership. ## 3. Oxford Analytica — Ownership History

### Bottom line
Oxford Analytica has changed hands **twice** since 2021, both **CONFIRMED (a) via official, on-the-record, dual-sided press releases**, plus independent corroboration from UK Companies House PSC filings. **Neither transaction is a 2026 event** — the most recent is dated March 2025 — so this should be flagged to the analyst as **historical relative to today (2026-09-07), not a fresh 2026 data point.** No 2026 ownership event was found for Oxford Analytica specifically.

### Ownership chain (chronological)

**1975–2021: Independent, founder-controlled.**
- Founded 1975 by **David R. Young** (a former Nixon White House aide), as a private company built around a network of expert-contributor political/economic risk analysis (the "Daily Brief" product).
- David R. Young (the founder) died; multiple in-memoriam pieces dated **January 2026** were found (Richard Nixon Foundation blog, Oxford Analytica's own site) — this is a leadership/founder-biography data point, not an ownership-structure event, but flagged since it's a genuinely recent (Jan 2026) item about the company.
- UK Companies House PSC register (which only goes back to 2016, when the PSC regime began, so nothing before 2016 shows in this register) shows, prior to 2021: **Mr David Kelly Depauw Young** (b. Nov 1980 — a different, younger person from founder David R. Young; likely a relative but this was not independently verified) and **The Oxford Analytica International Group Inc** (Delaware corporation) as the controlling persons, notified 6 April 2016. A third historical PSC, **Dr George Austin Stroup**, was notified 6 April 2016 and ceased 3 March 2017.
- No evidence of private-equity ownership was found for this period (the brief hinted PE might be part of Oxford Analytica's history; searches for `"Oxford Analytica" "private equity" investor stake` did not surface any PE-firm-specific ownership — only generic/unrelated finance-industry results). **Treat "Oxford Analytica has had PE ownership" as unconfirmed** absent further evidence.

**17 February 2021: FiscalNote acquires Oxford Analytica.** Status **(a)**.
- Confirmed via FiscalNote's own newsroom release ("FiscalNote Announces Acquisition of Oxford Analytica," fiscalnote.com) and a GlobeNewswire syndication.
- **Deal value: not disclosed** in the release.
- Quotes on record: Tim Hwang (CEO, FiscalNote) — "Oxford Analytica has earned its stellar reputation as the pioneer of geopolitical risk analysis"; David K. Young (CEO, Oxford Analytica at the time) — "I could think of no better team than the one Tim has put together at FiscalNote."
- Strategic context per the release: followed FiscalNote's 2018 purchases of CQ Roll Call and a Brussels-based firm.
- **Companies House corroboration:** PSC register shows **The Oxford Analytica International Group, LLC** (Delaware) notified as PSC **12 February 2021** — and ceased the same day — with the prior Delaware-corp/individual PSCs (above) also ceasing 12 February 2021. The near-simultaneous notify/cease pattern on the same date is consistent with a same-day restructuring/closing around the FiscalNote deal, though the notes here don't have visibility into exactly why the LLC entry shows as both notified and ceased on 12 Feb 2021 — flagged as a filing-mechanics nuance, not a red flag on the deal itself, which is independently well-confirmed via the press releases.
